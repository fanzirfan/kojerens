About KOJERENS ASCII Matrix

KOJERENS ASCII Matrix is a high-performance generative canvas laboratory tool that converts images and video media into vector ASCII and Unicode glyph matrices directly in your browser.

Designed & Developed by Fanz Irfan | manji.eu.org

How to Use

  1. Upload an image, video, or connect your webcam
  2. Tune image levels, glyphs, color maps, and density in the controls panel
  3. Preview in real-time and export as high-res PNG, pure text, or video sequence ZIP

Core Highlights

  • 100% Client-Side In-Browser Processing (Zero Server Dependencies)
  • Multi-language Unicode character sets (Latin, Cyrillic, Devanagari, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, etc.)
  • Hardware-accelerated Bloom, Photographic Exposure, and Gradient Mapping
  • Animated sequence rendering and webcam recording
