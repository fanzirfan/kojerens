// CORE ARCHITECTURE

Built for Precision & Privacy

Traditional online design tools send your private imagery to remote servers. KOJERENS executes all computer vision algorithms locally within your browser context.

Zero Server Footprint

100% client-side execution. Your photos, videos, and camera feeds remain in local memory and never touch an external cloud server.

Hardware Accelerated

Offscreen canvas buffers paired with requestAnimationFrame scheduling deliver fluid 60fps to 120fps slider response times even at 4K resolution.

Dynamic Workspaces

Adaptive icon rails and sliding panel drawers maximize your visual canvas area, keeping complex generative parameter controls accessible yet uncluttered.

Production Exports

Export transparent PNG overlays, full resolution branded posters, animated frame sequences in ZIP archives, and raw ASCII plaintext ready for production.

The KOJERENS Creative Manifesto

In an era of generic AI templates and bloated subscriptions, KOJERENS returns control to procedural rules, mathematical aesthetics, and direct pixel manipulation.

Whether generating sci-fi interface assets for film, topographic geodetic charts for editorial prints, or retro-futuristic ASCII motion clips, each tool in this suite is purpose-built to inspire new visual languages.
